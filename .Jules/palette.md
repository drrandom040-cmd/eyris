## 2026-06-27 - Search Form Flow Optimization
**Learning:** Optimizing search forms with `ImeAction.Next` for primary fields and `ImeAction.Search` for the final field, combined with trailing clear buttons and explicit keyboard dismissal, significantly improves the multi-field search experience on mobile.
**Action:** Always implement sequential focus navigation (`ImeAction.Next`) and contextual action triggers (`ImeAction.Search`) for search-intensive forms in Jetpack Compose.
