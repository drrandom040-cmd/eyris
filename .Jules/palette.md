## 2025-01-24 - Optimizing Multi-Field Search UX
**Learning:** In multi-field search forms, coordinating `ImeAction.Next` for intermediate fields and `ImeAction.Search` for the final field, combined with trailing clear buttons, creates a seamless 'flow' that feels both professional and highly accessible.
**Action:** Always implement `KeyboardOptions` and `KeyboardActions` alongside `trailingIcon` clear buttons for multi-input search screens in Jetpack Compose.
