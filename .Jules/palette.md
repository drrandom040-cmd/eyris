## 2025-02-15 - [Unified Semantics and Emoji Decoupling for TalkBack in Card Layouts]
**Learning:** Raw emojis (like 📞, 📷, 🎵, 💬) and character shortcuts (like 'f') used in dense layout components are pronounced in a literal, non-contextual, or unlocalized manner by screen readers (e.g., "camera", "speech balloon"). Merging children elements or clearing and setting exact localized semantic content descriptions prevents fragmented and noisy reader outputs while supporting full internationalization.
**Action:** Always decouple visual representation from screen reader reading by using `Modifier.clearAndSetSemantics { contentDescription = stringResource(id) }` on emoji indicators or short text indicators in interactive grid/list items, and explicitly configure the parent component's clickable modifier with standard `role = Role.Button` and contextual `onClickLabel`.
