## 2026-06-18 - [Search Usability & Social Accessibility]
**Learning:** Social handle indicators (emojis) in Jetpack Compose require explicit semantic content descriptions to be accessible. Furthermore, a smooth search experience on mobile is achieved by linking fields with ImeAction.Next and providing a clear dismiss/trigger on ImeAction.Search.
**Action:** Always include contentDescription for decorative but informative emojis and map keyboard actions to focus/keyboard controllers in multi-field forms.
