## 2025-05-14 - Optimized Search Form UX
**Learning:** Optimizing search forms with `ImeAction.Next` for intermediate fields and `ImeAction.Search` for the final field, combined with explicit keyboard dismissal and focus clearing on submission, significantly improves the 'flow' of multi-field search screens. Adding trailing clear buttons to text fields further enhances usability by allowing quick corrections.
**Action:** Apply this pattern to all multi-input search or filtering screens in the app to ensure consistent and professional interaction behavior.
