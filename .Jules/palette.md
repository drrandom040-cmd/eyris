## 2026-07-24 - [Accessible Jetpack Compose Emoji Indicators & merged semantics]
**Learning:** In Jetpack Compose, short characters or emojis (like 📞, f, 📷, 🎵, 💬) used as indicators inside clickable cards are pronounced literal or awkward names by screen readers. Merging card descendants with custom semantics must be carefully layered: top-level elements like category and star ratings benefit from a localized summary format, while lower-level emoji-based details require explicit `clearAndSetSemantics` overrides to prevent unhelpful and repetitive character-to-speech pronunciation.
**Action:** When designing information cards in dark premium themes with emoji shortcuts, always define translatable string descriptions and apply `clearAndSetSemantics` directly on the text-emoji elements, while explicitly defining the interaction `Role` and `onClickLabel` on the parent clickable element.
